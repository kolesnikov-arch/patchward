    What This Shows
    
          
    The verdict is formed from evidence the agent never controlled: a fix-blind acceptance test and a regression check. The agent's own test is always shown as passing — to make the point that a self-referential test cannot, on its own, be trusted. Internal scoring, calibration, and orchestration are not disclosed.
